How to read it

  • Double rectangle = weak entity set; the double diamond beside it is its identifying relationship.
  • Solid black triangle points at the parent in a subset hierarchy. Subsets inherit the parent's identifier, so they carry no identifier ellipse of their own.
  • Underlined attribute = part of the identifier. Only identifiers are drawn as ellipses here; non-key attributes are listed in the README to keep the sheets legible.
  • Arrowheads mark the "one" side. No arrows = M:N, one arrow = many-one, two arrows = one-one.
  • Four entities — Organization, Tournament, Teams, Creators — appear on more than one page. Those are the same entity set drawn twice, not duplicates.

One page · 25 relationships · 7 unavoidable line crossings

The whole schema on one sheet. Seven lines cross another, which is a property of the graph rather than the drawing — Tournament has degree 8 and Match, Teams and Contracts have degree 5, so no planar arrangement exists. The two longest edges (Engages and PaidToTeam) are routed around the outside margin rather than through the middle, which is what keeps the count as low as seven; v1 has roughly fifteen.
